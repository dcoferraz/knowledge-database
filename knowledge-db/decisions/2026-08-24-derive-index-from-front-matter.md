## Summary

INDEX.md is build output regenerated by `kb index` from entry front-matter; `kb check` (KB007) fails unless the file matches the generated output byte-for-byte. Hand-maintained content survives only inside `kb:manual` fenced regions (Ready-Answer Table, external docs).

## Context / Question

INDEX.md hand-maintained beside front-matter = two sources of truth. In practice it drifted: 3 rows in this repo listed fewer tags than their entries, and evaluator deployments showed missing rows and stale dates. Options: (A) lint that INDEX matches entries and report diffs, (B) derive INDEX entirely from front-matter.

## Findings / What We Did

Chose (B). A checker that reports drift still asks a human/agent to reconcile two documents; a generator makes drift unrepresentable and kills four violation classes at once (missing rows, tag drift, stale dates, dangling links) at zero maintenance cost. The old `kb-lint --fix` approach was (A) and the drift happened anyway. Generator: knowledge-db/bin/kb:377-460; header warning: knowledge-db/INDEX.md:5-6.

Trade-off accepted: prose descriptions per tag were dropped (vocabulary now lists values from kb.config.json); the manual regions keep the two genuinely human tables.
